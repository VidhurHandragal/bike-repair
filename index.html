Tools at this station

Inventory based on the SARIS Bike Fixation stand specification provided for this project. Check the installed equipment before starting.

Separate floor-mounted pump

The project expects SARIS model 26246, a floor-mounted pump. The exact pump head, valve compatibility, and gauge features have not been physically verified. Follow the pump’s own markings and instructions; do not force a connection.

    About this project

    Built to help neighbors keep rolling.

    Better Bikes was created by Vidhur Handragal of Troop 287 as an Eagle Scout service project. It was created originally for University Preparatory Academy and the Cathedral of Faith, at 2315 Canoas Garden Avenue in San Jose, California.

    The website supports a public bicycle repair station with free, safety-conscious instructions for common repairs. The organizations named here describe the project’s original setting; their mention does not imply endorsement or an ongoing maintenance commitment.
