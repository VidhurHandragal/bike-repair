Separate floor-mounted pump

We expect a SARIS 26246 floor pump here, but nobody has checked its pump head, valve fit, or gauge. Go by the labels on the pump, and never force the head onto a valve.

    About

    Made so neighbors can keep their bikes running.

    Better Bikes was created by Vidhur Handragal of Troop 287 as an Eagle Scout service project. It was created originally for University Preparatory Academy and the Cathedral of Faith, at 2315 Canoas Garden Avenue in San Jose, California.

    The site goes with a public repair station and gives free instructions for common repairs. The places named above are where the project started. Naming them doesn't mean they endorse it or will keep maintaining it.
